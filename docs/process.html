Development process · 2026-09-05

You switch the loop on and leave.

liminal is a platform for automated music production and live steering — two products on the same primitives; the endless set ships first. It develops itself: an agent takes a card from the board, implements, proves, reviews, delivers and merges — then takes the next. When a milestone closes, it releases and writes the next specs. When nothing is workable, it researches the domain. It stops for one thing only: a decision that is yours.

One iteration

Nine steps, one card, and the next starts in the same turn

Click a step. Its hard rule is below. The two with a blue border are where you may appear — without blocking anything.

0 · Gates

Before choosing, the loop reads everything you did since the last iteration: chat messages, edited issues, moved cards, new comments, idea and research issues. Chat outranks everything. Then: a PR waiting only on heard: ok that got it → merge now. An unanswered human thread → that is the iteration. Red CI on main → that is the iteration. A card in Decision needed → stop.

🔴 Any gate may become the iteration. None is skipped.

The turn rule. Never end without the next iteration started — or without a short report saying why it stopped. If the session sleeps, /loop 3m wakes it in 3 minutes and it continues the iteration in flight. It never restarts one.

Every card entering Blocked or Decision needed is announced in the chat — card, why, what unblocks it. A card you send to Backlog leaves the queue; one you set to Ready jumps it; moving a card out of a parking column is read as your answer.

Course decisions

Autonomous on how. You own where.

A local doubt becomes a question, the card parks in Blocked, and the loop takes another card. A course decision stops the loop until you answer — so the project never takes a wrong turn on a guess.

The loop stops when the doubt…Example
changes a requirement or a principle in the plan"should the set also accept a local mp3 now?"
reopens or adds an ADRaudio placement, engine, LLM provider, language
defines a new milestone or reorders themthe planning iteration's proposal
costs money or adds an external account or servicea paid API, telemetry, hosting
touches user data, privacy or licensingstoring references, sample licences, opt-in analytics
would waste more than one card's work if guessed wronga schema change every generator depends on
has two answers that lead to different products"listener-first or producer-first UI?"
How it stops: the card goes to Decision needed with the question, the options, what each costs and the loop's recommendation; the chat gets the same in your language; the turn ends with a stop report. How it resumes: you answer in the chat or on the issue, or move the card out; the next gate 0 records the decision (ADR or plan edit if it deserves one) and continues.

Every "done when" becomes a row with evidence. Screens are proven on screen.

What "heard: ok" means

  1. The card has a criterion only the speakers can judge (listening: true).
  2. The loop merges what the machine proves, leaves this criterion pending, and tells you in the chat what to listen for and how to run it.
  3. It does not wait: it takes another card.
  4. You listen. Good → comment on the PR: heard: ok. The next gate 0 merges.
  5. Not good → comment what you heard. It becomes a review thread: the loop fixes and asks again.

Run like a company

Product, usability, research, releases — on a cadence

Open source, but with a strategy: who it is for, what wins them, what loses them, and how we measure. docs/product/strategy.md is the compass; docs/product/usability.md the flows and heuristics; docs/research/topics.md the agenda.
