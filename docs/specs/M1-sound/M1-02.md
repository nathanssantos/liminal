## Context

`@liminal/engine` is isomorphic (ADR-0002): it receives an `AudioContext`, loads a `Score`, plays
with Tone.js. This card makes it play in Node (headless, for tests) and leaves it ready for the
renderer (M1-04).

## What to do

- `createEngine({ context, score }) → Engine` with `play()`, `stop()`, `dispose()`,
  `position(): { bar, beat, tick }`, `on('bar' | 'stopped' | 'ended', cb)`.
- Instrument registry: the 9 score presets → Tone nodes (`MembraneSynth` for kick,
  `MetalSynth`/`NoiseSynth` for hat and clap, `MonoSynth` for bass-mono, sine `Synth` for sub,
  saw `PolySynth` for poly-saw, `FMSynth` for pad, `AMSynth` for lead, `NoiseSynth`). Effects:
  `filter` and `eq3` in v1; the others throw `unsupported-fx` with the kind.
- Lookahead scheduling over `Tone.Transport` from the clips; `filter.cutoff` and `gainDb`
  automation via `setValueAtTime`/`linearRampToValueAtTime` per `curve`.
- Tick → seconds conversion only here: `ticksToSeconds(tick, bpm)`.
- `dispose()` disconnects and disposes **every** node created; nothing stays hanging on the context.
- In Node: `import 'node-web-audio-api/polyfill'` **before** `await import('tone')` (see
  `rules.md › engine`). The test spins up a real polyfill `AudioContext`, with output.

## Done when

- [ ] in Node, `play()` on the fixture emits 16 `bar` events with `bar` 0…15 in order, and `ended` at the end — proof: real-clock test (≈ 30 s at 128 BPM; tagged `slow`).
- [ ] `position()` during bar 4 returns `bar: 4` — proof: test.
- [ ] `stop()` at bar 3 emits `stopped` and no `bar` afterwards — proof: test.
- [ ] `dispose()` leaves the context with no engine nodes — proof: created × disposed count in the internal registry, test asserting zero pending.
- [ ] `filter.cutoff` of the `chords` track at bar 8's tick has the automation point's value — proof: test reading `AudioParam.value` at that instant.
- [ ] **listening:** running `pnpm --filter engine play:fixture` in the terminal, one hears a kick on 4, hats on eighths, bass and chords — proof: the human says "ok".

## Out of scope

Offline render (M1-03), renderer (M1-04), sampler, delay/reverb/compressor/distortion.

## Risks and questions

- `MetalSynth` as a hat may sound bad; if the human rejects it, swap the preset for `NoiseSynth`
  with a short envelope and note it in `rules.md`. The card does not close without the "ok".
- A 30 s real-clock test is slow; running the test fixture at `bpm: 400` cuts it to ≈ 10 s without
  changing the logic.
